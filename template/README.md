# Template

> Lightweight templates for recording contributions, reflecting on progress, and documenting projects or experiments.

Use these templates when they are useful. You don't need to use all of them.

- [`daily-log.md`](daily-log.md): Record a small contribution or action
- [`weekly-reflection.md`](weekly-reflection.md): Reflect on the week
- [`monthly-reflection.md`](monthly-reflection.md): Step back and look for patterns
- [`project-log.md`](project-log.md): Track a project's direction and progress
- [`experiment-log.md`](experiment-log.md): Capture a technical experiment and what you learned
