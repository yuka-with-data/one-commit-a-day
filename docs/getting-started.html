Getting Started

You don't need a perfect plan or a complicated system. Start with one small contribution and build from there.

Start with the Basics

1. Get Your Own Copy

Fork this repository on GitHub or clone it locally to create your own working space.

You can adapt the structure, templates, and workflow to fit your needs.

2. Start with the Yearly Logs

Open the current year's folder under logs/.

It provides a ready-to-use structure for daily, weekly, and monthly records. 

logs/
└── 2027/
    ├── README.md
    ├── calendar.md
    ├── daily/
    ├── weekly/
    └── monthly/

You can begin with just the daily logs. The other sections can come later.

3. Make One Small Contribution

Choose something meaningful that moves a project or your learning forward.

For example:

  • Improve documentation or fix a typo.
  • Explore an issue or review a pull request.
  • Investigate a bug or learn something new.
  • Make progress on your own project.

It doesn't have to be a Git commit.

4. Record What Happened

Use the daily log to capture what you worked on, what you learned, or what you contributed.

Keep it simple. A few sentences are enough.

5. Explore More When You're Ready

As your practice develops, explore the additional journals and templates.

  • journal/ — Explore your direction and TO-BE goals.
  • templates/ — Reusable formats for different activities.
  • logs/ — Track your progress and reflect over time.

Start Small. Grow at Your Own Pace.

You don't need to use every feature or follow a perfect routine. Make the framework work for you.
