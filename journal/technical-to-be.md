# 🌱 Technical TO-BE Journal

> A lightweight journal for thinking about who you want to become, not just what you want to acomplish.

Technology changes quickly. Tools, roles, and skills will change. This journal is a place to step back and think about what you want to remain true about you.

There are no right answers. Write what feels meaningful, and revisit it as you grow.

## 1. Why Tech?

- Why am I in tech?
- What keeps me interested?
- What do I enjoy building, learning, or exploring?

> What makes me want to be here?

## 2. What Matters to Me?

- What problems do I want to solve?
- Who do I want to help?
- What makes technical work meaningful to me?
- What values do I want my work to reflect?

> What do I want my work to be about?
