# 🌱 Technical TO-BE Journal

> A lightweight journal for thinking about who you want to become, not just what you want to accomplish.

Technology changes quickly. Tools, roles, and skills will continue to change. This journal is a place to step back and think about what you want to remain true as you grow.

There are no right answers. Write what feels meaningful, and revisit it as you grow.

## 1. Why Tech?

- Why am I in tech?
- What keeps me interested?
- What do I enjoy building, learning, or exploring?

> What makes me want to be here?

## 2. What Matters to Me?

- What problems do I want to solve?
- Who do I want to help?
- What makes technical work meaningful to me?
- What values do I want my work to reflect?

> What do I want my work to be about?

## 3. My Inner Compass

It's easy to shape our goals around what other people, the industry, or society expects from us.

**自分軸 (Jibunjiku)** is about developing your own inner compass: knowing what matters to you and making choices that reflect it.

Take a moment to ask:

- What do I genuinely want, apart from what I feel I should want?
- Which of my goals come from my own interests, values, and curiosity?
- Are there expectations from the industry, community, peers, family, or society influencing my direction?
- Which of these influences do I agree with, and which do I want to question?
- If I stopped comparing my path with others, what would I still want to pursue?

> Is my direction coming from my own values, or from the surrounding expectations?

### My Own Direction

> What do I want to stay true to, regardless of outside expectations?

## 4. Who Do I Want to Become?

- How do I want to think and approach problems?
- How do I want to work with others?
- What do I want people to be able to rely on me for?

> What kind of technical person do I want to become?

## 5. My Technical TO-BE

Complete whichever prompts resonate with you. You don't need to answer them all.

- I want to become someone who...
- I want to build...
- I want to solve...
- I want to contribute...
- I want to be known for...

### My TO-BE

> I want to become a technical person who...

## 🔄 Revisit

Come back periodically and ask:

- What has changed?
- What still feels true?
- Am I becoming the person I described?
- Is my direction still aligned with what matters to me?
- What do I want to change or explore next?
