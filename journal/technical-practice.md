# 🛠️ Technical Practice Journal

> A lightweight journal for reflecting on how you want to practice, work, learn and contribute in the technical field.

Knowing what you want to become is one thing. How you practice every day is how you move toward it.

This journal is about **the way you want to work**, not a list of things you need to accomplish.

## 1. How Do I Want to Learn?

- What kind of learner do I want to be?
- What do I want to understand deeply rather than simply know how to use?
- How do I want to approach things I don't understand?

> How do I want to learn?

## 2. How do I Want to Work?

- How do I want to approach problems?
- How do I want to balance speed, quality, and quantity?
- How do I want to respond when things don't work?
- What habits or practices help me do my best technical work?

> How do I want to work?

## 3. How Do I Want to Contribute?

Technical work rarely happens in isolation.

- How do I want to contribute to projects and communities?
- How do I want to communicate with other people?
- What do I want others to be able to rely on me for?
- What kind of collaborator do I want to be?

> How do I want to contribute?

## 4. Putting It Into Practice

The way we practice becomes the evidence of who we are becoming.

Think about your current habits and experiences:

- What am I already doing that reflects the person I want to become?
- What could I practice more intentionally?
- What patterns do I want to change?
- What small actions help me move in the right direction?

> What can I practice today that reflects the technical person I want to become?

## 🔄 Revisit

As you gain experience, come back and reflect:

- What is changing in the way I practice?
- What am I learning about how I work and learn best?
- What do I want to practice differently next?
