# 🛠️ Technical Practice Journal

> A lightweight journal for reflecting on how you want to practice, work, learn and contribute in the technical field.

Knowing what you want to become is one thing. How you practice every day is how you move toward it.

This journal is about **the way you want to work**, not a list of things you need to accomplish.

## 1. How Do I Want to Learn?

- What kind of learner do I want to be?
- What do I want to understand deeply rather than simply know how to use?
- How do I want to approach things I don't understand?

> How do I want to learn?

## 2. How do I Want to Work?

- How do I want to approach problems?
- How do I want to balance speed, quality, and quantity?
- How do I want to respond when things don't work?
- What habits or practices help me do my best technical work?

> How do I want to work?
